Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 8, 2026

Important: Tapix is an offline-first Point of Sale (POS) application. All your business data is stored locally on your device and is NOT sent to our servers or any third parties.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Tapix POS ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and business data. This Privacy Policy explains how Tapix handles information when you use our mobile and desktop application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Tapix stores the following business data locally on your device only:

  • Product Information: Product names, descriptions, SKUs, barcodes, prices, costs, stock quantities, and product images
  • Sales Data: Sale invoices, customer purchases, payment methods, and transaction history
  • Purchase Data: Purchase orders, supplier invoices, and stock receiving records
  • Customer Information: Customer names, phone numbers, addresses, and account balances (if you choose to record them)
  • Supplier Information: Supplier names, contact details, and payment records
  • Employee Data: Employee names, roles, salaries, and work schedules (if you use the employee management feature)
  • Financial Records: Expenses, journal entries, and accounting data
  • User Credentials: Login usernames and encrypted passwords for app access control

2.2 Data We DO NOT Collect

Tapix does NOT collect, transmit, or store any of your business data on external servers. We do not have access to:

  • Your sales records
  • Your customer information
  • Your financial data
  • Your product inventory
  • Any other business-related information

2.3 Technical Information (Optional)

If you choose to activate license verification, we may collect:

  • License Key: To verify your subscription status
  • Device Identifier: A unique anonymous ID to prevent license sharing
  • App Version: To ensure compatibility and provide updates

Note: This information is only used for license validation and does NOT include any of your business data.

3. How We Use Information

3.1 Local Data Usage

All business data you enter into Tapix is used exclusively on your device to:

  • Manage your inventory and products
  • Process sales and purchases
  • Generate financial reports
  • Track customer and supplier accounts
  • Manage employee information and payroll

3.2 License Verification

If you have an active subscription, we use your license key to:

  • Verify your subscription status
  • Prevent unauthorized use
  • Provide app updates and support

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

All your business data is stored in an encrypted SQLite database on your device. This data:

  • Remains on your device at all times
  • Is protected by your device's security features
  • Is accessible only through the Tapix app with valid login credentials
  • Can be backed up by you to your preferred location (local or cloud)

4.2 Data Security Measures

  • Password Encryption: User passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms
  • Database Encryption: Sensitive data is encrypted at rest
  • No Cloud Sync: Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or backup

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do NOT share, sell, or transmit your business data to any third parties.

Tapix operates entirely offline. Your data is yours and stays on your device.

5.1 Camera and Photo Library Access

Tapix requests access to your device camera and photo library for the following purposes only:

  • Camera: To scan product barcodes and QR codes
  • Photo Library: To select product images and company logos

These permissions are optional. If denied, you can still use the app with manual barcode entry and without product images.

6. Your Data Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • Access: You can access all your data anytime through the app
  • Export: You can export your data to CSV, Excel, or PDF formats
  • Delete: You can delete individual records or clear all data through the app settings
  • Backup: You can create backups and restore them at any time
  • Portability: You own your data and can transfer it to other systems

7. Children's Privacy

Tapix is a business application not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an in-app notification (for significant changes)

9. International Users

Tapix can be used worldwide. Since all data is stored locally on your device, data protection laws of your country apply to how you manage your business information.
